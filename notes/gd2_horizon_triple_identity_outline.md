# Geometric Duality II: The Horizon Triple Identity

## Abstract

This paper is the geometric completion of *Geometric Duality of Black-Hole Interiors*. GD1 introduced the central bridge: a flat FLRW cosmology can be rewritten in Painlevé-Gullstrand form using the areal radius \(r=a(t)R\), producing the flow term \(F=Hr\). GD2 develops the claim that the de Sitter curvature scale \(L\), the causal horizon \(\mathcal H\), and the timelike matching shell \(\Sigma\) are not independent ingredients, but distinct presentations of one underlying throat structure.

The goal is not to erase the mathematical differences among these objects. \(L\) is a curvature scale, \(\mathcal H\) is a null or observer-causal horizon structure, and \(\Sigma\) is a timelike matching worldtube. The thesis is that in the GD construction these are different faces of the same horizon-centered geometry.

GD1 established the initial bridge between black-hole interiors and cosmological expansion. GD2 asks what underlying structure makes that bridge coherent.

## 4. Causal Track: Horizon Crossing and Interior Standpoint

The causal track begins with the Alice/Bob thought experiment.

Alice crosses the horizon in finite proper time and experiences no local singularity at the crossing. Bob, by contrast, sees asymptotic freezing and redshift. The same event is therefore locally smooth for Alice and globally horizon-structured for Bob.

The key lesson is:

> horizons are not first-order local objects; they are global causal structures.

This motivates the possibility that what appears externally as collapse may be read internally as cosmological evolution.

## 5. Geometric Track: FLRW, PG Flow, and de Sitter Embedding

The geometric track is the exact backbone.

Start from spatially flat FLRW:

\[
ds^2=-dt^2+a(t)^2(dR^2+R^2d\Omega^2).
\]

Define

\[
r=a(t)R.
\]

Then

\[
dr=a\,dR+Hr\,dt,
\]

so

\[
a\,dR=dr-Hr\,dt.
\]

Substitution gives

\[
ds^2=-dt^2+(dr-Hr\,dt)^2+r^2d\Omega^2.
\]

This is the PG flow form with

\[
F=Hr.
\]

In the pure de Sitter limit, the same structure follows from the 5D Minkowski embedding of the 4D de Sitter hyperboloid. Thus the PG flow is not merely a coordinate trick; in the de Sitter sector it is naturally generated by the embedding geometry.

## 6. The Matching Shell

The matching shell \(\Sigma\) is a timelike 3D hypersurface, not a spatial slice and not literally the null horizon.

On the shell:

\[
R=1, \qquad r=a(\tau).
\]

At each fixed \(\tau\), the shell has a 2-sphere cross-section. As \(\tau\) evolves, these spheres trace a timelike worldtube.

The shell is the history-bearing seam where the interior FLRW/PG description is matched to the exterior Schwarzschild-de Sitter description. Its privileged status comes from continuity of the induced metric and extrinsic curvature at the background level.

## 7. Curvature Scale, Horizon, and Shell

GD2 should define the three objects carefully.

### The curvature scale \(L\)

\[
L=\sqrt{\frac{3}{\Lambda}}.
\]

This is the de Sitter curvature radius. It is a geometric invariant scale of the de Sitter spacetime.

### The causal horizon \(\mathcal H\)

\(\mathcal H\) is the observer-causal or null-access face of the same scale. In pure de Sitter, every geodesic observer sees the same horizon radius:

\[
R_H=\frac{c}{H}=L.
\]

### The matching shell \(\Sigma\)

\(\Sigma\) is the timelike worldtube where the interior and exterior descriptions are glued. It is not literally the null horizon, but it tracks the distinguished boundary scale through time.

## 8. The Horizon Triple-Identity Hypothesis

### Weak reading

The conservative statement is that the relevant scales coincide in the pure de Sitter or symmetric limit:

\[
R_H=L.
\]

The matching shell is placed at the corresponding distinguished scale.

### Strong reading

The stronger GD hypothesis is:

> the curvature scale, the causal horizon, and the matching shell are different presentations of one underlying throat structure.

Equivalently:

> the throat is geometric when read as curvature, causal when read through null access, and timelike when read as the evolving junction seam.

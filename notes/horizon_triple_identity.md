# Retired: The Horizon Triple-Identity Hypothesis

*Archived organising note · retired July 31, 2026*

This note originally proposed the shorthand

\[
L\leftrightarrow\mathcal H\leftrightarrow\Sigma
\]

for a possible relationship among a de Sitter curvature scale, an observer-causal horizon, and a matching history. The shorthand is retired because it invites a false identification. These are different kinds of geometric objects:

- \(L\) is an invariant curvature scale;
- \(\mathcal H\) is a specified null causal boundary;
- \(\Sigma\) is a matching hypersurface, matching history, or finite transition region.

In exact de Sitter space, the curvature radius, apparent-horizon radius, and cosmological event-horizon radius have the same numerical value. That symmetric-limit coincidence is not an identity of the underlying objects. At finite matter or radiation density, even the associated horizon radii separate.

The current research question is therefore constructive rather than identificatory:

> Can one 5D throat geometry derive the relationships among its distinct curvature, causal, and junction structures?

## The conjecture, first

We put the conjecture before the mathematics, because it — not the calculations — is what would decide whether this touches the problem you pose.

> **A black hole interior is the succeeding aeon.** For an observer crossing a horizon, the interior spatial scale and the exterior areal radius are reciprocal, \(a(\tau)=1/r(\tau)\). The parent's approach to \(r\to0\) is therefore the child's expansion to \(a\to\infty\); the parent's collapse is the child's Big Bang.

Under this single posit three things that CCC introduces by hand become consequences. The conformal reciprocation you impose at the crossover is *produced* by infall geometry rather than assumed. The low-Weyl Big Bang is *inherited* rather than fine-tuned. And black holes are not obstacles the construction must dispose of — they are the crossover itself.

Everything below is either the map from this conjecture onto your framework, or evidence that the crossover it demands is geometrically regular. We are interested in your judgment of the conjecture. The calculations are only there so that the judgment is about physics and not about whether the bookkeeping closes.

## The map onto CCC

Your crossover is governed by a reciprocation of the conformal factor: the factor that compactifies one aeon's future infinity is inverted to become the factor that expands the next aeon's Big Bang. In our reading this is exactly \(a=1/r\), realized at a horizon rather than posited at an abstract boundary. The reciprocal is not decorative: it is what turns a place of unbounded curvature in one description (the parent's \(r\to0\)) into a smooth, infinite future in the other (the child's \(a\to\infty\)).

We are candid that \(a=1/r\) is a posit — it is the founding move of the programme, not a theorem. But it is a *narrow* posit. Within the class of scale-free maps that fix the horizon, the inverse-radius law is the unique non-trivial involution; the power is forced by that symmetry, not chosen. What remains genuinely open is one level deeper: why the parent–child exchange should be a scale-free, horizon-fixing involution at all. That is the conjecture's soft underbelly, and we would rather you saw it plainly than have it buried.

## Why the Big Bang is low in Weyl — with nothing tuned

This is the part meant for your riddle, and it is a conjecture, so we state it without hedging and then say exactly why we believe it.

A universe in this picture is born from the collapse of a parent overdensity, at the finite epoch when its horizon forms — not, we should correct an earlier misstatement of our own, at the parent's future infinity. A *smooth* overdensity has a smooth interior: homogeneous dust collapse (Oppenheimer–Snyder) has an interior that is exactly Friedmann, with Weyl identically zero. A broad, low-amplitude overdensity therefore collapses to a low-Weyl child. The child's Big Bang is smooth because the thing it collapsed from was smooth.

Now close the loop. Low-amplitude perturbations produce broad, shallow overdensities; those collapse to low-Weyl children; low-Weyl children carry small perturbations. **Low Weyl at birth is a fixed point of the recursion.** There is no first, absurdly special moment to account for — the low-entropy condition is the self-reproducing attractor of the map, transmitted down the lineage. The improbability you quantify as one part in \(10^{10^{123}}\) is, in this reading, not improbable: it is what a universe born from a smooth collapse generically is.

We do not claim to have *proved* the fixed point converges rather than relocating the tuning up the lineage. That convergence is the deepest open question here, and it is the one on which the whole claim to have addressed your riddle stands or falls.

## What the conjecture lets you drop

Three features of CCC that draw the most fire are, in this picture, unnecessary.

**Evaporation.** You require black holes to have evaporated before the crossover so that their singular Weyl does not spoil the conformal boundary. Here they need not evaporate at all: each is a child aeon, and its singularity is replaced by that child's smooth future.

**Information destruction.** With the interior a genuine successor universe, information is not lost at a singularity — it passes into the child. The black hole's entropy is not erased from the ledger; it becomes the child's available phase space (its de Sitter horizon), so the newborn universe begins far below its ceiling. The arrow follows without an anti-unitary step.

**Rest-mass decay.** You need matter to become massless for conformal invariance at \(I^+\). Here the crossover is a horizon, not a null infinity that every worldline must reach as massless radiation; matter crosses by falling through, so we do not need mass to fade. We flag this as the least developed of the three — it is a change of setting more than a completed argument.

## The calculations (that it is coherent, not merely appealing)

One objection is fatal if unanswered: a black hole sitting on the conformal boundary carries a Weyl scalar that diverges, which is precisely why you must remove them. We can show it need not.

On the reciprocal interior the residual Weyl scalar dilutes as \(a^{-2}\) — the conformal weight of the Weyl scalar itself. Under the de Sitter compactification (\(\Omega\propto1/a\)) the *rescaled* Weyl is therefore finite and non-zero at the boundary, while the *physical* Weyl vanishes. Both facts are what one wants: the physical Weyl \(\to0\) is your hypothesis satisfied — a clean, low-entropy surface — and the finite rescaled Weyl is the gravitational information carried across to seed the next aeon. The exponent is not adjustable; \(a^{-2}\) is the unique dilution for which the boundary is at once regular and information-bearing (\(a^{-3}\) would carry nothing across; a shallower power would diverge). We have checked this directly against the Weyl tensor on several metrics.

Two supporting results sit under it: the anisotropic stress a smooth throat requires is fixed at \(\Pi=-\tfrac12 D\) (half the density contrast), and the child's de Sitter horizon entropy equals the parent's black-hole entropy under \(\Lambda=3/R_S^2\), so the two horizons are one thermodynamic object. Neither is essential to the conjecture; both are evidence the crossover is a real surface with consistent data.

## The honest ledger

So that you can weigh it as a builder would:

**Conjecture — the load-bearing posits.** That \(a=1/r\) is the physical content of the crossover; that a black-hole interior is the succeeding aeon; that low Weyl at birth is a converging fixed point of the recursion. These are what solve the riddle if anything here does. They are not derived.

**Calculation — the coherence proof.** The conformal weight \(a^{-2}\) and its finite rescaled seed; the throat stress \(\Pi=-\tfrac12 D\); the horizon-entropy identity. These are checked, and they are what turn the conjecture from a story into a construction that closes.

**Open.** What forces the reciprocal involution; whether the fixed point provably converges; where the child's matter and radiation come from (the transition microphysics), which we can currently only constrain, not derive.

One thing we will not oversell: the primordial perturbation spectrum this yields is adiabatic and near-scale-invariant — consistent with the sky, but degenerate with inflation, not a distinguishing prediction. The wider programme's empirical wager lives elsewhere, on a galaxy-scale acceleration law, and we keep it out of this note deliberately. What we bring *here* is the geometry of the low-entropy beginning, which is your question, not ours to have asked first.

## The invitation

We would rather learn from you that this fails than believe on our own that it works. Three places we most want your eye:

1. Is \(a=1/r\) a legitimate physical realization of your conformal reciprocation, or is there a geometric obstruction to identifying an infalling interior with a succeeding aeon that we have not seen?
2. Does the fixed-point argument genuinely dissolve the low-entropy fine-tuning, or does it relocate it up the lineage under another name?
3. Is "the black hole's entropy becomes the child's phase space" a coherent replacement for information loss, or does the accounting fail across the crossover?

If any of the three is wrong, the conjecture does not address your problem, and we would like to know that cleanly.

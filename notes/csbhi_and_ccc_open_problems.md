## Abstract

The crossover notes place CSBHI's child-birth mechanism in the same family as Penrose's Conformal Cyclic Cosmology: a regular future conformal boundary continued as a low-Weyl Big Bang. That association is an asset only if CSBHI does not simply inherit CCC's well-known unsolved problems. This note takes CCC's five standard objections in turn and asks, honestly, which the reciprocal construction *cures*, which it *reframes*, and which it *inherits*.

The scorecard: one clean cure (the rest-mass problem), one partial cure (conformal-factor uniqueness), one candidate cure that depends on a separate conjecture (entropy reset), one genuine reframing into a testable form (anisotropy growth), and one non-issue that CSBHI simply does not depend on (the disputed Hawking-point evidence). The cures are structural consequences of two CSBHI-specific facts — that its future boundary is a genuine de Sitter \(\mathscr I^+\), and that its conformal map \(a=1/r\) is *derived and unique* — not new assumptions bolted on.

The value of the exercise is defensive: it lets CSBHI borrow CCC's mature crossover language without borrowing its liabilities, and it marks exactly which liabilities remain shared.

## 1. CCC's five contested points

Conformal Cyclic Cosmology joins the infinite future of one aeon to the conformally stretched Big Bang of the next across a spacelike crossover surface [1,2]. Its standing criticisms, in decreasing order of severity [P]:

1. **Rest-mass decay.** The crossover requires the far future to be *massless* so the physics is conformally invariant and the rescaling maps physical to physical. This needs all rest mass to eventually fade — for which there is no known mechanism.
2. **Conformal-factor non-uniqueness.** There is no agreed prescription for the conformal factor \(\Omega\) across the crossover; competing proposals disagree with Penrose and with each other.
3. **Entropy reset.** The spent aeon's entropy must be shed for the next to begin low; Penrose invokes information *destruction* at black-hole evaporation, which breaks unitarity as usually understood.
4. **Weyl Curvature Hypothesis / anisotropy.** The low-Weyl boundary is unproven, and quantum backreaction could *amplify* anisotropies across cycles rather than smoothing them.
5. **Hawking points.** The observational support (concentric low-variance CMB circles) was re-evaluated to ~1σ and is not statistically significant [3].

---

## 2. Rest-mass decay — cured

**CCC's burden.** The *same* matter must cross the boundary conformally, hence be massless; CCC must decay all rest mass to reach that state.

**CSBHI's escape [C].** Its crossover surface is a genuine **de Sitter future conformal infinity**, which is conformally regular as a standard result — no mass-decay assumption is needed to make \(\mathscr I^+\) smooth. Under the reciprocal expansion \(a\to\infty\), ordinary matter and radiation *dilute* (\(\rho_m\propto a^{-3}\), \(\rho_r\propto a^{-4}\to0\)) to dynamical irrelevance, and \(\Lambda\) dominates. The parent-patch matter is not required to become massless; it is required only to become *negligible in density*, which unbounded expansion guarantees. The child's matter is then generated *fresh* at its own hot Big Bang, so nothing massive must survive the rescaling.

**The trade [O].** The rest-mass problem is exchanged for a **matter-genesis problem** — what sources the child's hot Big Bang at \(\Sigma\) (the transfer law of the crossover). This is the more tractable, precedented question: particle production at a crossover/bounce is standard in Einstein–Cartan and bounce cosmologies. Verdict: **cured**, with the burden shifted to a well-posed successor problem.

---

## 3. Conformal-factor uniqueness — partial cure

**CCC's burden.** No unique prescription for \(\Omega\).

**CSBHI's position [C].** Inside the reciprocal patch the map is not chosen but *derived*: \(a=1/r\) is the unique scale-free horizon-fixing involution (the involution result). CCC's "which conformal factor" complaint is therefore answered for the interior scaling. **However**, the crossover factor \(\Omega\) at \(\Sigma\) is a distinct object from the interior map \(a=1/r\), and it is not yet fixed. Verdict: **partial** — the interior map is unique; the boundary map is still open [O].

## 4. Entropy reset — candidate cure, conditional on the entropy identification

**CCC's burden.** Shed a spent aeon's entropy without a mechanism, or by destroying information.

**CSBHI's candidate [C].** Developed in the [gravitational-entropy note](note.html?src=gravitational_entropy_and_conformal_scaling): if gravitational entropy is a conformally-covariant quantity (area/Weyl), it is **scale-relative**, and each universe measures it from its own self-dual minimum \(a=1\). The parent's large final and the child's small initial gravitational entropy sit at different conformal scales related by \(\Omega\); there is no quantity to reset and no information to destroy. Verdict: **candidate cure**, strictly conditional on the entropy-identification conjecture holding and reproducing the horizon-entropy numbers. Not independent of that note [O].

---

## 5. WCH / anisotropy amplification — reframed into a test

**CCC's burden.** The low-Weyl boundary is assumed; anisotropies might grow across cycles.

**CSBHI's reframing [C/O].** The same danger appears as a *concrete dynamical question*: does the reciprocal interior isotropize (shear driven to zero — an attractor) or BKL-amplify (shear grows toward the boundary)? Because the bare Schwarzschild interior is Kantowski–Sachs and generically Weyl-dominated, this is not automatic; it is the recursion-stability problem flagged in the crossover note and the WCH thread. Verdict: **not cured, but converted** from a hypothesis into a shear-evolution calculation with a definite pass/fail. This is the single most important open item shared with CCC.

---

## 6. Hawking points — not a dependency

**CCC's burden.** Its most-cited observational support is disputed to non-significance [3].

**CSBHI's position [E].** CSBHI does not rest on the CMB-circle evidence; its own candidate observables are independent (e.g. the evolving galactic acceleration scale and its high-redshift test). The Hawking-point dispute is simply not a CSBHI dependency. Verdict: **non-issue** — sidestepped by not relying on it.

## 7. Scorecard and residuals

| CCC objection | Severity to CCC | CSBHI outcome | On what it depends |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rest-mass decay | High | **Cured** | de Sitter \(\mathscr I^+\) regular by standard asymptotics; child matter generated fresh |
| Conformal-factor uniqueness | Medium | **Partial** | interior map derived (\(a=1/r\)); crossover \(\Omega\) still open |
| Entropy reset | High | **Candidate cure** | conditional on the gravitational-entropy identification |
| WCH / anisotropy | High | **Reframed** as attractor test | reciprocal-interior shear evolution (KS vs BKL) |
| Hawking points | Medium | **Non-issue** | CSBHI uses independent observables |

**The residuals that remain, ranked.** (i) The matter-genesis / crossover transfer law at \(\Sigma\) (§2's trade). (ii) The crossover conformal factor \(\Omega\) (§3). (iii) The reciprocal-interior isotropization proof (§5) — the load-bearing one, since a BKL-amplifying interior would break the recursion regardless of everything else. (iv) The numerical test of the entropy identification (§4).

**Honest framing.** None of the "cures" is a completed calculation; each is a structural argument with a named successor problem. What the note establishes is narrower and still useful: CSBHI's association with CCC is *net favorable* — it imports CCC's crossover language while its two distinctive ingredients (a genuine de Sitter \(\mathscr I^+\); a derived, unique conformal map) retire or reframe CCC's worst liabilities rather than inheriting them. Whether the successor problems close is the content of the crossover and entropy notes, not of this comparison.

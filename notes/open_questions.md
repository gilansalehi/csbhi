# What Remains Mathematically Illegible

*A living index of open questions for CSBHI*

## Purpose

A part of the theory is **mathematically legible** when its objects are defined, its assumptions are visible, and its equations determine what happens next. An unanswered question is not automatically illegible: some questions require only further calculation. Illegibility begins where the theory still lacks the geometric object, dynamical law, boundary condition, or observable that would make the calculation unique.

CSBHI now has a legible conditional interior. Given the reciprocal ansatz

\[
a(\tau)=\frac{1}{r(\tau)},
\]

Alice's isotropic metric, Einstein's equations, and ordinary matter–radiation–vacuum stress-energy produce a definite expansion history and its reciprocal infall law. The junction calculations also establish several exact constraints on any exterior-to-interior completion.

The questions below identify what remains open. They distinguish missing foundations from ordinary extensions so that a long research programme does not look like one undifferentiated gap.

## What is already legible

The present project supplies:

- the reciprocal ansatz and its kinematic consequences;
- the field equations of the specified reciprocal interior;
- matter and radiation dilution under the reciprocal map;
- finite-curvature de Sitter asymptotics and singularity deferral within that interior;
- the local timelike FLRW–Kottler matching conditions;

These results are conditional on the specified interior and do not yet constitute a complete parent–child spacetime.

## I. Load-bearing geometric questions

### Q1. What selects the reciprocal ansatz?

**Known:** The relation \(a=1/r\) produces the central infall–expansion correspondence and preserves the characteristic matter, radiation, and vacuum powers.

**Missing:** A derivation of the reciprocal power from a symmetry, variational principle, embedding, or boundary condition. At present the ansatz is proposed rather than forced.

**Closure test:** Derive \(a=1/r\), or a tightly constrained equivalence class containing it, from the completed throat geometry without inserting the reciprocal relation as an independent premise.

### Q2. What is the full 5D throat geometry?

**Known:** The vacuum-dominated child interior has the standard 5D de Sitter embedding and an exact Painlevé–Gullstrand flow in areal-radius coordinates.

**Missing:** A metric extending that asymptotic result through the matter and radiation eras while also producing the parent-side geometry.

**Closure test:** Exhibit a higher-dimensional metric and its relevant four-dimensional slices or projections; calculate their induced geometries; and show that they recover Bob's exterior, Alice's reciprocal interior, and the correct de Sitter limit.

### Q3. What supports the parent–child transition?

**Known:** The local timelike dust–\(\Lambda\) junction closes cleanly. The simplest direct stationary null soldering matches intrinsically but leaves transverse curvature. That jump equals the child-side work density projected from volume to area.

**Missing:** The finite geometry, stress-energy, or higher-dimensional curvature that carries this work without attaching it to an otherwise discontinuous metric.

**Closure test:** Compute the completed throat's Einstein tensor and junction data; identify its matter or geometric source; test conservation, energy conditions, perturbative stability, and the thin-throat limit.

### Q4. What is the horizon and clock map?

**Known:** Alice's cosmological event-horizon radius obeys

\[
H_A=\frac{c+\dot B}{B}.
\]

Candidate calculations also map a parent crossing label \(v_H\) to an interior comoving label \(R=\Psi(v_H)\).

**Missing:** A derivation of \(B(\tau)\), \(\Psi(v_H)\), and the synchronization between Bob's exterior time and Alice's proper time from one parent geometry.

**Closure test:** Produce the maps from the throat metric, preserve null incidence and causal ordering, and recover the reconstructed event-horizon history without feeding the observed \(H_A(a)\) back into the construction.

## II. Completing the cosmological model

### Q5. What fixes the absolute reciprocal normalization?

**Known:** Standard observations determine scale-factor ratios. The reciprocal proposal makes the horizon crossing \(r=a=1\) a potentially physical normalization rather than a mere convention.

**Missing:** The crossing epoch, its relation to the hot Big Bang, and the amount of expansion between the throat and today. Conditional thermal calibrations do not yet derive that interval.

**Closure test:** Derive the absolute clock from the parent collapse and throat history, then obtain today's \(a_0\), the corresponding parent coordinate \(r_0\), and the parent mass–child \(\Lambda\) relation without choosing the crossing landmark afterward.

### Q6. Where do the interior fluids come from?

**Known:** Given independently conserved matter, radiation, and vacuum components, their equations of state and dilution laws follow conventionally.

**Missing:** A mechanism that produces those components, their initial abundances, entropy, and near-thermal radiation state from the parent collapse or throat transition.

**Closure test:** Derive the child stress-energy and its conservation law from the completed geometry, including the transition into a hot radiation-dominated epoch.

### Q7. How do perturbations cross and evolve?

**Known:** The present calculations describe only the homogeneous background and idealized radial congruences.

**Missing:** Scalar, vector, and tensor perturbations; their junction conditions; the origin of near-scale-invariant fluctuations; and the growth of structure.

**Closure test:** Derive the perturbation equations and transfer functions, then calculate the CMB power spectra, matter power spectrum, gravitational-wave propagation, and lensing observables.

### Q8. Does the model reproduce the full thermal history?

**Known:** The adopted background can be calibrated to the observed matter–radiation–\(\Lambda\) expansion history.

**Missing:** A first-principles account of inflation or an alternative causal mechanism, reheating, baryogenesis, nucleosynthesis, recombination, and neutrino decoupling.

**Closure test:** Recover the measured light-element abundances, acoustic scale, last-scattering history, and other early-universe observables with one consistent parameter set.

### Q9. Does geometry solve the vacuum-energy problem or only reframe it?

**Known:** CSBHI relates the observed effective \(\Lambda\) to a horizon-scale curvature and reproduces the familiar Planck-to-horizon hierarchy geometrically.

**Missing:** An explanation of why quantum vacuum contributions do not renormalize the effective cosmological constant away from that geometric value.

**Closure test:** Show how the completed theory treats vacuum stress-energy and why radiative corrections preserve, cancel, or fail to gravitate against the derived curvature scale.

### Q10. What observation distinguishes CSBHI from \(\Lambda\)CDM?

**Known:** At the homogeneous background level, the construction deliberately reproduces much of the successful matter–radiation–\(\Lambda\) history.

**Missing:** A robust observable that differs from \(\Lambda\)CDM and cannot be absorbed into initial conditions or parameter choices.

**Closure test:** Produce a quantitative prediction for perturbations, horizon-scale correlations, black-hole/cosmology relations, gravitational waves, galaxy dynamics, or another measurable sector before fitting the relevant data.

## III. Generality and later consequences

### Q11. Does the construction survive beyond spherical symmetry?

**Known:** The current model uses spherical symmetry and nonrotating Schwarzschild or Kottler parents.

**Missing:** Rotating and charged parents, realistic collapse, accretion, mergers, and nonspherical perturbations.

**Closure test:** Extend the throat and junction construction to Kerr-like geometry and show which reciprocal results remain invariant.

### Q12. Is singularity deferral global and stable?

**Known:** Within the specified reciprocal FLRW interior, \(r\to0\) becomes an infinite-proper-time, finite-curvature de Sitter future.

**Missing:** A proof that the completed parent–child geometry is geodesically complete and does not restore a curvature singularity, Cauchy horizon, or unstable mass-inflation region elsewhere.

**Closure test:** Construct the maximal extension, draw its global causal structure, evaluate curvature invariants and geodesic completeness, and test the result under perturbations.

### Q13. Can the galaxy-scale law be derived dynamically?

**Known:** Scale-free self-dual mixing selects the conditional relation

\[
g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_NcH},
\]

which has the form needed for MOND-like phenomenology and the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation.

**Missing:** A covariant field equation or throat-response law producing that scaling, including lensing, clusters, and relativistic systems.

**Closure test:** Derive the weak-field equations from the completed geometry and fit rotation curves, lensing, cluster dynamics, and cosmological structure without adding an unconstrained interpolation law.

### Q14. What becomes of horizon entropy and information?

**Known:** The transverse work term has an exact horizon-thermodynamic representation, and parent horizon area laws offer a candidate dynamical input.

**Missing:** The microscopic interpretation of throat entropy, the mapping of parent information into child degrees of freedom, and compatibility with unitary black-hole evolution.

**Closure test:** Derive the entropy and information map from the same degrees of freedom that support the throat, rather than adding thermodynamics as an analogy after the geometry is chosen.

## Dependency order

The questions do not all have equal priority:

\[
\boxed{
Q1+Q2
\longrightarrow
Q3+Q4
\longrightarrow
Q5+Q6
\longrightarrow
Q7+Q8+Q9+Q10
}
\]

Questions 11–14 test the construction's generality and reach. They matter, but they cannot repair a missing throat geometry. The immediate mathematical frontier is therefore narrow: derive the reciprocal map and its junction from one 5D parent–child construction.

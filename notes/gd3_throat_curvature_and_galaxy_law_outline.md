# Geometric Duality III: Throat Curvature and the Galaxy Acceleration Law

## Abstract

This paper is the dynamical payoff of the Geometric Duality program. GD1 establishes the black-hole/cosmology bridge by rewriting flat FLRW in Painleve-Gullstrand form. GD2 develops the Horizon Triple Identity, treating the de Sitter curvature scale \(L\), the causal horizon \(\mathcal H\), and the matching shell \(\Sigma\) as different presentations of one throat structure.

GD3 asks whether this same throat geometry explains the galaxy acceleration problem. The target is the MOND/BTFR-like scaling

\[
g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_N cH},
\]

which for a point mass implies

\[
v^4\sim GMcH.
\]

The present outline records the existing selection arguments and the missing dynamical step.

GD3 should be the first true downstream prediction paper.

## 2. Core Target

The core target is

\[
g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_N cH}.
\]

For a point mass,

\[
g_N=\frac{GM}{r^2}.
\]

Then

\[
g_{\rm mix}(r)
\sim
\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r^2}cH}
=
\frac{\sqrt{GMcH}}{r}.
\]

Equating this to centripetal acceleration gives

\[
\frac{v^2}{r}=\frac{\sqrt{GMcH}}{r},
\]

so

\[
v^4\sim GMcH.
\]

This is the BTFR scaling in the GD setting.

## 3. Route A: Curvature Mixing

The first route treats the law as a geometric mean of local and global curvature scales.

Local source curvature may be estimated as

\[
k_M\sim\frac{g_N}{c^2}.
\]

The global horizon curvature scale is

\[
k_H\sim\frac{H}{c}.
\]

The simplest mixed throat acceleration is then

\[
g_{\rm mix}\sim c^2\sqrt{k_Mk_H}
\sim c^2\sqrt{\frac{g_N}{c^2}\frac{H}{c}}
\sim\sqrt{g_NcH}.
\]

This route is physically intuitive: the galaxy feels not only its local baryonic curvature but also the global horizon curvature supplied by the throat structure.

## 4. Route B: Recursive Throat Self-Duality

The second route uses a symmetry principle.

Assume the local baryonic sector and the global horizon sector are two presentations of the same throat structure at different scales. Then a scale-free monomial mixed law has the form

\[
g_{\rm mix}\propto g_N^\alpha(cH)^{1-\alpha}.
\]

If the theory is self-dual under local/global exchange, then

\[
\alpha=1-\alpha,
\]

so

\[
\alpha=\frac12.
\]

Therefore the exchange-symmetric law is

\[
g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_NcH}.
\]

a_H:=cH.

Restoring \(a_H=cH\),

They do not yet identify the exact dynamical object that realizes the law.

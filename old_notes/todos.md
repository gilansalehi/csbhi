Here’s what I, as a seasoned GR/cosmology “skeptic,” would need to see before buying into Geometric Duality as more than a very clever coordinate & matching trick:

---

### 1. A fully worked-out perturbation theory that reproduces the CMB

* **Derive** linearized perturbation equations around your matched PG–FRW patch (including radiation, baryons, dark matter) in Painlevé–Gullstrand form.
* **Show** that collapse-seeding (realistic stellar asymmetries) maps into an almost scale-invariant, Gaussian, adiabatic spectrum $P(k)$ with the right tilt $n_s$ and amplitude $A_s$.
* **Compute** the CMB TT/EE/TE power spectra and demonstrate sub-percent agreement with Planck, including acoustic peak ratios, damping tail, polarization patterns and tensor limits.

If you can reproduce ΛCDM’s precision Cℓ’s purely from collapse-driven inhomogeneities inside your FRW patch, that would be a smoking-gun.

---

### 2. A credible hot-Big-Bang realization (BBN through recombination)

* **Embed** a multi-fluid (radiation + matter + Λ) interior in the same junction and show no extra shell stresses arise.
* **Track** the temperature $T(a)=T_0/a$ from an initial post-collapse value down through $T\sim$ MeV—demonstrating the correct neutron-to-proton ratio and light-element yields at BBN.
* **Evolve** photon–baryon acoustic oscillations in your PG–FRW frame through recombination ($z\sim1100$), recovering the same sound horizon and BAO signature.

Meeting ΛCDM’s success on BBN abundances and BAO in galaxy surveys would seal the deal.

---

### 3. Non-linear structure formation & observations

* **Extend** to non-linear dynamics (N-body or halo-model) to show galaxy clustering, weak-lensing statistics and cluster counts come out right.
* **Check** that your collapse-origins scenario doesn’t inject spurious anisotropies or large-scale features that aren’t observed.

If large-scale structure “just works” without new free parameters, you’ve got a viable competitor.

---

### 4. A robust astrophysical collapse → universe mapping

* **Demonstrate** (analytically or with numerical relativity) that a realistic, asymmetric stellar collapse generically produces the interior PG–FRW patch with the correct mass, radius and initial conditions—no fine-tuning.
* **Show** that the junction at $r=1$ and the inversion $r\to1/a$ emerge naturally from the dynamics of collapse.

In short, I want to see that this isn’t just “we glue in an FRW patch by hand,” but that collapsing matter *really* gives you the FRW universe on the inside.

---

### 5. A unique, testable prediction

Finally, I’d look for **one or two observational signatures** that ΛCDM wouldn’t predict—perhaps subtle non-Gaussianities traced back to collapse-mode spectra, or a correlation between black-hole populations and large-scale anomalies.  If GD can point to something measurable that differs even slightly from standard inflationary predictions, and that signature is borne out by data, I’d be fully convinced.

---

**Bottom line:** matching the zero-mode (background expansion) is just the start.  To persuade the broader community—and me—you need the full perturbation machinery, the thermal history, structure formation all falling naturally out of a collapse scenario, and ideally a “smoking-gun” prediction that LCDM can’t mimic.

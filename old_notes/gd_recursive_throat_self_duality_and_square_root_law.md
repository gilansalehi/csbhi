# GD Recursive Throat Self-Duality and the Square-Root Law

## Purpose

This note records a new argument for why the MOND-like GD mixing law should take the geometric-mean form

\[
g_{\rm mix} \sim \sqrt{g_N cH}.
\]

The argument does not begin from dimensional convenience alone. It begins from the stronger GD hypothesis that the same throat structure reappears recursively across scales.

---

## 1. Starting point

The current GD program already points toward a triple identity:

\[
L \leftrightarrow \mathcal H \leftrightarrow \Sigma,
\]

where the de Sitter curvature radius, the causal horizon, and the matching shell are read as different manifestations of one underlying throat structure.

A further possibility is that this throat structure is not unique to the universe as a whole, but recurs across nested black-hole/cosmological levels.

In that case, local and global sectors are not fundamentally different ontological categories. They are the same geometric structure viewed at different levels of scale.

---

## 2. Recursive black-hole cosmology as a selection principle

Suppose the universe is itself a black-hole-like throat geometry, while also containing black holes that instantiate the same throat logic internally.

Then the theory becomes recursively self-similar.

The local baryonic sector and the global cosmological sector should not enter the mixed law asymmetrically in any fundamental way. If the underlying geometry is truly recursive, the mixing law should be invariant in form under exchange of the two sectors.

That is the key new selection principle.

---

## 3. General scale-free mixed law

Assume the weak-field mixed acceleration depends only on:

- the local Newtonian acceleration \(g_N\),
- the global throat acceleration scale \(cH\),
- and no new dimensionful scale.

Then the most general monomial scale-free ansatz is

\[
g_{\rm mix} \propto g_N^{\alpha} (cH)^{1-\alpha}.
\]

---

## 4. Self-duality under local/global exchange

If recursive throat symmetry means that the local and global sectors are presentations of the same structure at different levels, then the mixed law should be symmetric in form under exchange:

\[
g_1 \leftrightarrow g_2.
\]

Applied to the monomial family,

\[
f(g_1,g_2) \propto g_1^{\alpha} g_2^{1-\alpha},
\]

exchange symmetry requires

\[
\alpha = 1-\alpha.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\alpha = \tfrac12.}
\]

So the mixed law is uniquely fixed to be

\[
\boxed{g_{\rm mix} \sim \sqrt{g_N cH}.}
\]

---

## 5. Consequence for rotation curves

Using

\[
g_N = \frac{GM}{r^2},
\]

we obtain

\[
g_{\rm mix}(r)
\sim \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r^2}cH}
= \frac{\sqrt{GMcH}}{r}.
\]

Equating this to the centripetal acceleration \(v^2/r\) yields

\[
\frac{v^2}{r} = \frac{\sqrt{GMcH}}{r},
\]

hence

\[
v^2 = \sqrt{GMcH},
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{v^4 = GMcH.}
\]

This is the MOND/BTFR scaling law in the GD setting.

---

## 6. Why this is stronger than the earlier scale argument

The earlier argument for the square root rested on:

- scale-freeness,
- dependence only on \(g_N\) and \(cH\),
- and the requirement of flat outer rotation curves.

The present argument adds a stronger principle:

- **recursive self-duality of throat structure across scales.**

That means the exponent \(1/2\) is not just the value that works observationally. It is the value selected by symmetry under exchange of local and global sectors when those sectors are understood as nested realizations of the same geometry.

---

## 7. Status of the argument

This argument is stronger than a purely dimensional guess, but it is still not yet the final tensorial derivation.

What it achieves:

- it explains why the square root is the uniquely natural exponent,
- it ties that exponent to the strong GD ontology,
- and it embeds the MOND law into recursive black-hole cosmology rather than treating it as an isolated empirical fit.

What it does not yet achieve:

- it does not yet identify the exact tensor, invariant, projected curvature term, or weak-field metric correction whose dynamics realize this exchange symmetry.

So this note should be read as a **self-duality selection argument** for the square-root law.

---

## 8. Working interpretation

A strong reading is:

> If GD describes a recursive black-hole cosmology in which throat structure reappears across scales, then the mixed acceleration law must be scale-free and self-dual under exchange of local and global sectors. That symmetry uniquely selects the geometric mean \(g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_N cH}\).

This may be the cleanest conceptual route yet to explaining the MOND exponent within the GD framework.

---

## 9. Consequence for roadmap

This result suggests the project has advanced in an important way.

The missing ingredient is no longer merely “why a square root?” in the abstract. The question is now narrower:

> what exact geometric or dynamical object realizes the recursive self-duality that selects the square root?

That sharpened target should guide the next phase of derivation work.

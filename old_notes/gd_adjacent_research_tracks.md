# Adjacent Research Tracks: Citation and Reference Landscape

This document maps the research landscape adjacent to Geometric Duality (GD). Each track is described in terms of what it contributes and where it stands in relation to the GD program. The goal is to provide a citation backbone and a map of intellectual debts, competitors, and convergences.

---

## Track 1: Black Hole Thermodynamics and the Holographic Principle

This is the foundational backdrop for the thermodynamic side of GD. The central insight — that black-hole horizons carry entropy proportional to their area — motivates treating the cosmic horizon as an information-bearing boundary.

**Bekenstein, J.D. (1972, 1973)**
- *"Black holes and entropy"*, Phys. Rev. D 7, 2333 (1973)
- *"Black holes and the second law"*, Lett. Nuovo Cim. 4, 737 (1972)
- Established that black holes carry entropy `S ~ A/4` in Planck units. This is the seed of all subsequent horizon thermodynamics. GD's cosmic horizon is an entropy-bearing surface in Bekenstein's sense.

**Hawking, S.W. (1974, 1975)**
- *"Black hole explosions?"*, Nature 248, 30 (1974)
- *"Particle creation by black holes"*, Commun. Math. Phys. 43, 199 (1975)
- Showed that black holes radiate thermally at temperature `T = ħc³/(8πGMk_B)`. Established that horizons are physical thermodynamic objects, not just coordinate artifacts.

**Gibbons, G.W. and Hawking, S.W. (1977)**
- *"Cosmological event horizons, thermodynamics, and particle creation"*, Phys. Rev. D 15, 2738 (1977)
- Extended Hawking's result to de Sitter spacetime: the cosmological horizon radiates at temperature `T = H/(2π)` (in natural units). This is the key result connecting the GD throat geometry to horizon thermodynamics. The GD cosmic acceleration scale `a₀ ~ cH` is directly related to this temperature.

**'t Hooft, G. (1993)**
- *"Dimensional reduction in quantum gravity"*, gr-qc/9310026
- Proposed that the physics of a volume can be encoded on its bounding surface — the holographic principle. This motivates treating the GD matching shell as an information-theoretic boundary.

**Susskind, L. (1995)**
- *"The world as a hologram"*, J. Math. Phys. 36, 6377 (1995)
- Extended and sharpened the holographic principle. Together with 't Hooft, this provides the conceptual scaffolding for the thermodynamic track of GD.

**Maldacena, J. (1997)**
- *"The large N limit of superconformal field theories and supergravity"*, Int. J. Theor. Phys. 38, 1113 (1999); hep-th/9711200
- The AdS/CFT correspondence: an explicit realization of holography relating a bulk gravitational theory to a boundary conformal field theory. GD is not AdS/CFT, but it asks a similar question in de Sitter rather than anti–de Sitter. The dS/CFT program (see Track 3) is the more direct cousin.

**Bousso, R. (2002)**
- *"The holographic principle"*, Rev. Mod. Phys. 74, 825 (2002)
- Comprehensive review of holography and the covariant entropy bound. Good reference for situating GD's thermodynamic horizon claims.

## Track 2: Spacetime Thermodynamics and Emergent Gravity

GD's thermodynamic track is most directly adjacent to this body of work. These authors derive gravitational dynamics from thermodynamic or information-theoretic principles at horizons.

**Jacobson, T. (1995)**
- *"Thermodynamics of spacetime: the Einstein equation of state"*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 75, 1260 (1995)
- Derives the Einstein field equations from the first law of thermodynamics `δQ = TδS` applied to local Rindler horizons. This is arguably the cleanest derivation of GR from horizon thermodynamics. GD is in conversation with this: the throat is a privileged Rindler-like horizon for infalling observers.

**Verlinde, E. (2010, 2016)**
- *"On the origin of gravity and the laws of Newton"*, JHEP 2011, 029 (2011); arXiv:1001.0785
- *"Emergent gravity and the dark universe"*, SciPost Phys. 2, 016 (2017); arXiv:1611.02269
- First paper: derives Newtonian gravity from entropic considerations on holographic screens.
- Second paper: the key GD-adjacent work. Proposes that the de Sitter horizon acts as an entropic reservoir whose response to baryonic matter produces an extra acceleration in the deep `g < a₀` regime, recovering the MOND-like `g_eff ~ √(g_N cH)` law.
- GD's relationship: GD agrees with the horizon insight and the scale `a₀ ~ cH`, but provides a concrete geometric throat structure (the matching shell / embedded hypersurface) explaining *why* the horizon is dynamically relevant. Verlinde's framework is thermodynamic; GD is geometric.

**Padmanabhan, T. (multiple)**
- *"Thermodynamical aspects of gravitation"*, Rep. Prog. Phys. 73, 046901 (2010)
- *"Emergent perspective of gravity and dark energy"*, Res. Astron. Astrophys. 12, 891 (2012)
- *"Distribution function of the atoms of spacetime and the nature of gravity"*, Entropy 17, 7420 (2015)
- Padmanabhan repeatedly treats gravity as emergent from spacetime "atoms" at causal boundaries, and interprets cosmological evolution as holographic bulk-boundary bookkeeping. His concept of "holographic equipartition" connects cosmic expansion to a balance of bulk and boundary degrees of freedom.
- GD's relationship: The throat geometry in GD may provide the concrete geometric realization of the boundary Padmanabhan is gesturing at.

## Track 3: de Sitter Holography and Cosmological Horizons

This track addresses what holography means in de Sitter space — directly relevant to GD's embedding picture.

**Strominger, A. (2001)**
- *"The dS/CFT correspondence"*, JHEP 0110, 034 (2001); hep-th/0106113
- Proposes a holographic duality between de Sitter gravity and a CFT living on the spacelike boundary at future infinity. Less well-established than AdS/CFT (de Sitter has no obvious boundary CFT), but the most direct holographic analogue in the positive-Λ setting.

**Witten, E. (2001)**
- *"Quantum gravity in de Sitter space"*, hep-th/0106109
- Discusses difficulties of holography in de Sitter. Useful contrast with the AdS case.

**Banks, T. (2000s)**
- Various papers on the finite-entropy nature of de Sitter and its implications for quantum gravity. The finite Gibbons-Hawking entropy `S_dS ~ 3π/GΛ` is a hard constraint any de Sitter cosmology must respect.

## Track 4: Penrose — Singularity Theorems, Cosmic Censorship, and Conformal Geometry

Penrose's work is foundational for GD on multiple levels: singularity theorems constrain what GD's singularity-deferral mechanism must evade; CCC offers a structurally similar but different conformal boundary picture.

**Penrose, R. (1965)**
- *"Gravitational collapse and space-time singularities"*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 14, 57 (1965)
- The first singularity theorem (for black holes): once a trapped surface forms, a singularity is unavoidable under energy conditions. GD's singularity-deferral mechanism must be understood in this context — it does not violate the theorem but reinterprets what the singularity *means* from the interior point of view.

**Hawking, S.W. and Penrose, R. (1970)**
- *"The singularities of gravitational collapse and cosmology"*, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 314, 529 (1970)
- Extended to cosmology: the Big Bang singularity is unavoidable under similar energy conditions. GD's reinterpretation of the Big Bang as a horizon-crossing event rather than a curvature singularity must be squared with this result.

**Penrose, R. (1969)**
- *"Gravitational collapse: the role of general relativity"*, Riv. Nuovo Cim. 1, 252 (1969)
- Introduced the cosmic censorship conjecture: naked singularities do not form in generic gravitational collapse. Relevant to GD's shell matching and what happens at the Schwarzschild singularity in the exterior chart.

**Penrose, R. (2010)**
- *"Cycles of Time: An Extraordinary New View of the Universe"* (book); technical papers: *"Before the Big Bang: an outrageous new perspective"*, etc.
- Conformal Cyclic Cosmology (CCC): the universe undergoes infinite aeons, each beginning with a Big Bang and ending in a de Sitter-like future. The conformal rescaling connects consecutive aeons at the boundary.
- GD's relationship: structurally very different from GD (no black-hole interior picture), but CCC and GD both take the conformal/projective structure of de Sitter boundaries seriously. Penrose's conformal compactification techniques are useful tools.

## Track 5: Painlevé–Gullstrand Coordinates and the River Model

The exact technical foundation of the GD geometric track.

**Painlevé, P. (1921) and Gullstrand, A. (1922)**
- Original papers establishing the PG coordinate system for Schwarzschild. PG coordinates are horizon-regular (unlike Schwarzschild time), describe a freely-falling rain of observers, and make the flow structure transparent. These are the coordinates in which the GD duality is most naturally expressed.

**Hamilton, A.J.S. and Lisle, J.P. (2008)**
- *"The river model of black holes"*, Am. J. Phys. 76, 519 (2008); gr-qc/0411060
- A pedagogically clean treatment of Schwarzschild and Kerr spacetimes as "flowing space" in PG form. The river model makes the connection between PG flow and FLRW expansion visually and physically transparent. Very close in spirit to GD's infall function picture.

**Martel, K. and Poisson, E. (2001)**
- *"Regular coordinate systems for Schwarzschild and other spherical spacetimes"*, Am. J. Phys. 69, 476 (2001); gr-qc/0001069
- A reference on the family of horizon-regular coordinate systems including PG. Useful for locating GD within the standard literature.

## Track 6: Lemaître–Tolman–Bondi Collapse and Junction Conditions

These are the technical tools required for the off-shell extension of GD (Open Problem P7).

**Lemaître, G. (1933)**
- *"L'Univers en expansion"*, Ann. Soc. Sci. Brux. A 53, 51 (1933)
- First inhomogeneous dust collapse solution. The LTB metric is the natural candidate for the off-shell extension of GD's interior.

**Tolman, R.C. (1934)**
- *"Effect of inhomogeneity on cosmological models"*, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. 20, 169 (1934)

**Bondi, H. (1947)**
- *"Spherically symmetrical models in general relativity"*, Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 107, 410 (1947)

**Darmois, G. (1927)** and **Israel, W. (1966)**
- *"Singular hypersurfaces and thin shells in general relativity"*, Nuovo Cim. B 44, 1 (1966)
- The Israel junction conditions are used explicitly in GD's Appendix B. They govern when two spacetime regions can be glued without a thin-shell stress-energy source.

**Oppenheimer, J.R. and Snyder, H. (1939)**
- *"On continued gravitational contraction"*, Phys. Rev. 56, 455 (1939)
- The original exact solution for dust-ball collapse to a black hole. The Oppenheimer-Snyder (OS) interior is a closed FLRW universe matched to Schwarzschild exterior — structurally very close to GD's construction but run in reverse. GD can be read as the time-reversed OS collapse seen from the inside.

## Track 7: MOND, Relativistic Completions, and Galaxy Phenomenology

GD's galactic phenomenology track must be situated in this literature.

**Milgrom, M. (1983)**
- *"A modification of the Newtonian dynamics as a possible alternative to the hidden mass hypothesis"*, Astrophys. J. 270, 365 (1983)
- The original MOND papers (three-paper series, same journal issue). Postulates the acceleration scale `a₀ ~ 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s²` and the interpolation between Newtonian and deep-MOND regimes. GD derives the scale; the interpolation law remains an open problem (P2).

**Bekenstein, J.D. (2004)**
- *"Relativistic gravitation theory for the modified Newtonian dynamics paradigm"*, Phys. Rev. D 70, 083509 (2004); astro-ph/0403694
- TeVeS (Tensor-Vector-Scalar): the first complete relativistic extension of MOND. GD must eventually be compared with TeVeS on lensing and perturbation theory, where TeVeS has known difficulties.

**Famaey, B. and McGaugh, S.S. (2012)**
- *"Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND): Observational Phenomenology and Relativistic Extensions"*, Living Rev. Relativ. 15, 10 (2012)
- The comprehensive review of MOND phenomenology and theory. Essential reference for locating GD's galactic predictions.

**McGaugh, S.S., Lelli, F. and Schombert, J.M. (2016)**
- *"Radial Acceleration Relation in Rotationally Supported Galaxies"*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 117, 201101 (2016)
- The observational RAR: a tight empirical relation between observed and baryonic centripetal acceleration across galaxy types. This is the primary observational target for GD's galactic sector.

**Lelli, F., McGaugh, S.S. and Schombert, J.M. (2016)**
- *"SPARC: Mass Models for 175 Disk Galaxies with Spitzer Photometry and Accurate Rotation Curves"*, Astron. J. 152, 157 (2016)
- The dataset underlying the RAR. Any quantitative GD prediction for rotation curves should be tested against SPARC.

**Tully, R.B. and Fisher, J.R. (1977)**
- *"A new method of determining distances to galaxies"*, Astron. Astrophys. 54, 661 (1977)
- The original Tully-Fisher relation (luminosity–velocity). The baryonic version (BTFR) is the deep-MOND prediction `v⁴ ~ GMa₀` that GD derives from throat geometry.

**Sanders, R.H. and McGaugh, S.S. (2002)**
- *"Modified Newtonian Dynamics as an Alternative to Dark Matter"*, Annu. Rev. Astron. Astrophys. 40, 263 (2002)
- Review of MOND before the RAR. Still a useful survey.

**Mannheim, P.D. and Kazanas, D. (1989)**
- *"Exact vacuum solution to conformal Weyl gravity and galactic rotation curves"*, Astrophys. J. 342, 635 (1989)
- Conformal gravity: an independent modified gravity approach that also produces flat rotation curves geometrically. A structural comparison with GD is worthwhile.

## Track 8: Black Hole Cosmology — Universe Inside a Black Hole

This is the direct lineage for GD's cosmological reading.

**Frolov, V.P., Markov, M.A. and Mukhanov, V.F. (1988, 1990)**
- *"Through a black hole into a new universe?"*, Phys. Lett. B 216, 272 (1989)
- *"Black holes as possible sources of closed and semiclosed worlds"*, Phys. Rev. D 41, 383 (1990)
- Early formal proposals that black holes could spawn interior cosmologies. Sets the conceptual stage for GD.

**Smolin, L. (1992)**
- *"Did the universe evolve?"*, Class. Quant. Grav. 9, 173 (1992)
- Cosmological natural selection: black holes spawn new universes with slightly mutated constants. A philosophical ancestor of the GD program, though GD's mechanism is geometric rather than evolutionary.

**Easson, D.A. and Brandenberger, R.H. (2001)**
- *"Universe generation from black hole interiors"*, JHEP 0106, 024 (2001); hep-th/0103019
- Argues for universe creation inside black holes. Closer in spirit to GD than Smolin's version.

**Poplawski, N.J. (2010)**
- *"Cosmology with torsion: an alternative to cosmic inflation"*, Phys. Lett. B 694, 181 (2010)
- Proposes that torsion in Einstein-Cartan gravity causes each black hole to produce an interior big bounce / new universe. The interior cosmology is in many ways similar to GD's picture, but the mechanism is torsion-driven rather than geometric duality.

**Farrah, D. et al. (2023)**
- *"Observational Evidence for Cosmological Coupling of Black Holes and its Implications for an Astrophysical Source of Dark Energy"*, Astrophys. J. Lett. 944, L31 (2023)
- Claims observational evidence that black hole masses grow with cosmic expansion, interpreted as cosmological coupling. Controversial. The theoretical mechanism proposed overlaps conceptually with GD's horizon-geometry picture. Worth engaging critically.

## Track 9: Observational Cosmology — Constraints GD Must Satisfy

These are the observational anchors that any alternative to ΛCDM must reproduce.

**Perlmutter, S. et al. (1999)** and **Riess, A.G. et al. (1998)**
- *"Measurements of Ω and Λ from 42 High-Redshift Supernovae"*, Astrophys. J. 517, 565 (1999)
- *"Observational Evidence from Supernovae for an Accelerating Universe"*, Astron. J. 116, 1009 (1998)
- Nobel-winning discovery of cosmic acceleration. The primary observational motivator for Λ and the starting point for GD's dark energy reinterpretation.

**Planck Collaboration (2018, 2020)**
- *"Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters"*, Astron. Astrophys. 641, A6 (2020)
- The precision CMB data and ΛCDM parameter fits that any alternative must reproduce at the background level (done) and perturbation level (open problem P5).

**Eisenstein, D.J. et al. (2005)**
- *"Detection of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations in the Redshift Survey of 46,748 Luminous Red Galaxies"*, Astrophys. J. 633, 560 (2005)
- BAO detection. One of ΛCDM's three canonical successes (with SNe Ia and CMB). GD's perturbation theory must reproduce the BAO scale.

**Weinberg, S. (1989)**
- *"The cosmological constant problem"*, Rev. Mod. Phys. 61, 1 (1989)
- The classic statement of the cosmological constant problem: why is Λ so small compared to quantum field theory expectations? GD offers a geometric route to Λ that sidesteps the tuning, but must explain why the geometric value is so much smaller than the Planck scale.

## Track 10: Singularity Deferral and Quantum Gravity Alternatives

GD defers rather than resolves the singularity. These programs address singularity resolution more directly and provide contrast.

**Ashtekar, A. and Bojowald, M. (2006)**
- *"Quantum geometry and the Schwarzschild singularity"*, Class. Quant. Grav. 23, 391 (2006)
- Loop quantum cosmology (LQC) resolution of the Big Bang singularity via a bounce. In LQC the singularity is replaced by a quantum-gravity bounce. GD's approach is classical; this is the main quantum alternative.

**Bardeen, J.M. (1968)**
- *"Non-singular general relativistic gravitational collapse"*, in Proceedings of the International Conference GR5, Tbilisi, p. 174
- First regular (non-singular) black hole metric. Bardeen black holes replace the singularity with a de Sitter core. Structurally interesting in the GD context: the de Sitter interior appears in both pictures.

**Hayward, S.A. (2006)**
- *"Formation and evaporation of nonsingular black holes"*, Phys. Rev. Lett. 96, 031103 (2006)
- A regular black hole metric with a de Sitter center that transitions to Schwarzschild at large radius. The de Sitter core and the GD late-time de Sitter sector may be related.

## Track 11: Mach's Principle and Relational Gravity

Provides philosophical context for the GD claim that local dynamics are shaped by the global horizon structure.

**Mach, E. (1883)**
- *"Die Mechanik in ihrer Entwickelung"*
- The original statement: local inertia is determined by the global distribution of matter. GD resonates with this: the throat scale `a₀ ~ cH` is cosmological in origin but governs local galactic dynamics.

**Barbour, J.B. and Pfister, H. (eds.) (1995)**
- *"Mach's Principle: From Newton's Bucket to Quantum Gravity"*, Birkhäuser
- Comprehensive reference on Machian approaches. GD's claim that the MOND scale is set by the cosmic horizon is a specific realization of Machian coupling between local and global structure.

**Lynden-Bell, D. et al. (1995)**
- *"Mach's principle from the relativistic constraint equations"*, Mon. Not. R. Astron. Soc. 272, 150 (1995)

Geometric Duality is a long-form research programme exploring whether all the open questions in cosmology have a single answer.
ΛCDM is one of the most successful phenomenological models in modern science, but it leaves a constellation of deep questions unresolved. Geometric Duality treats these not as disconnected anomalies, but as clues pointing toward a single horizon-centered geometry connecting black-hole interiors, cosmic expansion, de Sitter curvature, and galaxy-scale dynamics, unifying these disparate concepts into one underlying spacetime picture: the Conformal Scaling of Black Hole Interiors.

Preamble

The programme begins with a single wager: that some of the deepest structures in modern cosmology may be read more clearly when FLRW expansion, Painlevé–Gullstrand flow, horizon matching, and de Sitter geometry are treated as parts of one geometric story.

Program

Current and forthcoming papers in the series.

The current plan is to build Geometric Duality in stages: first the black-hole/cosmology bridge, then the de Sitter embedding, then the throat identity, then the galaxy-scale square-root law.

  1. Paper I

    Geometric Duality of Black-Hole Interiors

    The entry paper. Rewrites flat FLRW in Painlevé–Gullstrand form, introduces the Alice/Bob horizon interpretation, and frames the universe as the interior of a black-hole-like geometry.

    draft 0.2

  2. Note I

    Painlevé–Gullstrand Flow from the 5D de Sitter Embedding

    Shows how the Painlevé–Gullstrand flow \(F=Hr\) arises from the 5D embedding of 4D de Sitter spacetime, and clarifies the relation between FLRW slicing, PG coordinates, and the de Sitter curvature scale \(L=\sqrt{3/\Lambda}\).

    draft note

  3. Note II

    The Horizon Triple Identity: Curvature Scale, Causal Horizon, and Matching Shell

    States the central pre-GD2 clarification: the de Sitter curvature scale \(L\), the causal horizon \(\mathcal H\), and the timelike matching shell \(\Sigma\) are not the same mathematical object, but may be different presentations of one underlying throat structure.

    planned

  4. Paper II

    Geometric Duality II: The Horizon Triple Identity

    The geometric completion paper. Synthesizes the causal Alice/Bob track with the exact FLRW-to-PG and de Sitter-embedding track, then develops the hypothesis that \(L\), \(\mathcal H\), and \(\Sigma\) are three faces of a single throat geometry.

    planned

  5. Note III

    Self-Dual Homogeneous Derivation of the Square-Root Law

    A theorem-style bridge to the galaxy problem. Shows that a scale-free, self-dual mixing law between local Newtonian acceleration \(g_N\) and the global throat scale \(cH\) naturally selects \(g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_NcH}\).

    technical note

  6. Paper III

    Geometric Duality III: Throat Curvature and the Galaxy Acceleration Law

    The dynamical payoff paper. Develops the galaxy-scale consequence of GD: local baryonic gravity and the global horizon scale mix through the throat geometry, producing a MOND/BTFR-like relation \(g\sim\sqrt{g_NcH}\) and \(v^4\sim GMcH\).

    planned

  7. Future

    Horizon Thermodynamics and the GD Interpretation

    A later synthesis paper connecting GD to horizon thermodynamics, emergent-gravity arguments, and the work of Verlinde, Padmanabhan, Milgrom, and McGaugh. The goal is to show how these approaches may be partial views of the same throat-centered geometry.

    future direction

This is a research programme, not a single claim. The aim is to build a coherent geometric ladder from horizon intuition to exact metric structure, and from exact metric structure to observable consequences.

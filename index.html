Geometric Duality is a long-form research programme exploring whether cosmology, horizon structure, and black-hole interior geometry are different presentations of one underlying spacetime picture: the conformal scaling of black hole interiors.

Preamble

The programme begins with a single wager: that some of the deepest structures in modern cosmology may be read more clearly when FLRW expansion, Painlevé–Gullstrand flow, horizon matching, and de Sitter geometry are treated as parts of one geometric story.

Programme

The current plan is to build Geometric Duality in stages: first the black-hole/cosmology bridge, then the de Sitter embedding, then the throat identity, then the galaxy-scale square-root law.

  1. Paper I

    Geometric Duality of Black-Hole Interiors

    The entry paper. Rewrites flat FLRW in Painlevé–Gullstrand form, introduces the Alice/Bob horizon interpretation, and frames the universe as the interior of a black-hole-like geometry.

    draft 0.2

  2. Note I

    Painlevé–Gullstrand Flow from the 5D de Sitter Embedding

    Shows how the Painlevé–Gullstrand flow \(F=Hr\) arises from the 5D embedding of 4D de Sitter spacetime, and clarifies the relation between FLRW slicing, PG coordinates, and the de Sitter curvature scale \(L=\sqrt{3/\Lambda}\).

    sketch

  3. Paper III

    The Horizon Triple Identity

    Formalizes the central throat claim: the de Sitter curvature scale, the observer horizon, and the timelike matching shell are not independent ingredients, but different presentations of one underlying horizon-throat structure.

    planned

  4. Paper IV

    The Square-Root Law

    Develops the galaxy-scale consequence of the throat picture: a self-dual mixed acceleration law \(g_{\rm mix}\sim\sqrt{g_N cH}\), yielding flat rotation curves and the baryonic Tully–Fisher scaling \(v^4\sim GMcH\).

    planned

  5. Paper V

    Horizon Thermodynamics and Geometric Synthesis

    Reconciles GD with horizon thermodynamics, emergent-gravity arguments, MOND phenomenology, and black-hole cosmology as partial views of the same geometric structure.

    prospective

The working thesis is that causal horizons, de Sitter curvature, matching shells, and galaxy-scale acceleration laws are different projections of one throat-centered spacetime geometry.

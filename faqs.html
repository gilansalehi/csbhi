How does Alice’s infall become isotropic cosmological expansion?

Bob assigns Alice a decreasing, horizon-normalized radius \(r(\tau)\). GD sets Alice's spatial scale to \(a(\tau)=1/r(\tau)\), so \(H_{\rm A}=\dot a/a=F(r)/r\). Applied equally in every spatial direction, this scale makes the probe cloud expand with \(\Theta=3H_{\rm A}\), zero shear, and zero vorticity. Alice and Bob both remain future-directed; the opposite signs come from differentiating the reciprocal.

Is \(a=1/r\) the same equation as \(\bar R=aR\)?

No. The equation \(\bar R=aR\) defines Alice's areal radius from her scale factor and comoving radial label. The ansatz \(a=1/r\) relates Alice's scale to the radius Bob assigns to her infall. At the horizon GD1 normalizes \(r=a=1\); choosing the comoving label \(R=1\) also gives \(\bar R=1\) for that labeled sphere. This shared value is a coordinate normalization, not a proof that the two radii are one coordinate.

Is Geometric Duality merely a coordinate transformation?

No. Vacuum Schwarzschild has anisotropic interior evolution and nonzero Weyl curvature, while Alice's FLRW interior is isotropic and conformally flat. No coordinate change followed by an ordinary scalar conformal rescaling can erase that difference. GD proposes a map between two geometries; deriving that map is the next construction problem.

Has the exterior-to-interior horizon map been derived?

Not yet. GD1 constructs Alice's reciprocal interior and its de Sitter endpoint. GD2 must determine whether a null junction, a timelike transition layer, or a common higher-dimensional throat connects it to Bob's exterior, and what stress-energy that construction requires.

Does the 5D de Sitter embedding prove the complete theory?

It proves a narrower exact result. In the pure-Λ limit, the standard 4D de Sitter hyperboloid produces the flat FLRW patch and its areal-radius PG flow \(F_{\rm dS}=H_\Lambda\bar R\). The embedding does not yet produce the full matter–radiation history or the parent-child horizon map.

Does GD assume FLRW expansion and then rediscover it?

Appendix A proves the reciprocal correspondence by mapping the established Friedmann background into the infall function. The companion field-equation note proves the stronger result: starting from Alice's reciprocal metric, Einstein's equations and stress-energy conservation produce the Friedmann dynamics before the calculation is translated into Bob's radial variable. The reciprocal ansatz remains the geometric input.

How does GD relate to earlier black-hole cosmologies and to ’t Hooft?

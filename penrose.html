A note for Sir Roger Penrose

The Successor Aeon as a Black-Hole Interior

Conformal Cyclic Cosmology as the interior geometry of black holes.

August 2026

Dear Sir Roger,

Conformal Cyclic Cosmology has, I believe, a realization inside black-hole physics that removes the commitments it must otherwise carry at the crossover. The identification is one line: black-hole collapse in one cosmological generation gives birth to a child universe in the next.

Collapse supplies the birth; reciprocal geometry supplies the evolution that follows. For an observer crossing the horizon, the interior spatial scale and the parent-side areal radius obey \(a=1/r\). The parent-side limit \(r\to0\) maps to the already-born child's expansion toward \(a\to\infty\): the exterior singular endpoint becomes a smooth conformal future in the interior description. Your conformal reciprocation \(\Omega\to1/\Omega\) therefore appears here as a horizon-anchored map within the child's history, not as its birth at future infinity.

The low-Weyl beginning then follows from collapse, not from selection. Homogeneous collapse — Oppenheimer–Snyder — has a Friedmann interior whose Weyl tensor vanishes identically even as its density diverges. A universe born from such a collapse inherits a smooth start from a black hole in the parent cosmological generation. Because a smooth universe is what forms the smooth overdensities that collapse into smooth children, low initial Weyl is a fixed point of the succession rather than an improbable choice. That is the Weyl Curvature Hypothesis, obtained as a property of the formation process rather than a condition placed upon it.

Three commitments Conformal Cyclic Cosmology carries at the crossover then become unnecessary. Matter need not become massless, because the generational passage occurs through collapse rather than through null infinity. Black holes need not have evaporated, because they are branching birth sites rather than debris awaiting a universal crossover. And the information need not be destroyed: where you are content to let the phase space contract at the singularity, one may instead let it re-open as the child's.

The other end of the child's history supplies a second result. On the reciprocal interior the residual Weyl dilutes as \(a^{-2}\), which is its conformal weight; under compactification the rescaled Weyl is finite at the child's future boundary while the physical Weyl vanishes. The same construction therefore gives collapse a low-Weyl birth and gives the resulting universe a regular conformal future. The property you secure by evaporation is secured instead by the interior geometry.

One conjecture remains: that the geometry of the succession forces Weyl smoothness on every aeon by self-consistency, rather than fixing it as a fine-tuned parameter. This is the claim I am now working to prove, in a small geometric program inspired by yours — the Conformal Scaling of Black Hole Interiors (gilansalehi.com/csbhi). More than anything, I hope you find it worth your time.

Sincerely,
Gilan Salehi

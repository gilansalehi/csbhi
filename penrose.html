A note for Sir Roger Penrose

The Successor Aeon as a Black-Hole Interior

Conformal Cyclic Cosmology as the interior geometry of black holes.

August 2026

Dear Sir Roger,

I believe the conformal succession you envisioned has a natural realization inside black-hole physics. Conformal Cyclic Cosmology and recursive black-hole cosmology may describe the same geometry at different scales: the universe approaches a smooth conformal future, while gravitational collapse within it opens branching child cosmologies. The Conformal Scaling of Black Hole Interiors supplies a reciprocal relation between the two descriptions.

The relation is \(a=1/r\). For an observer crossing a black-hole horizon, the interior spatial scale \(a\) is reciprocal to the parent-side areal radius \(r\). Collapse at finite parent time gives birth to the child; the reciprocal geometry carries the child's subsequent evolution. As the parent-side description approaches \(r\to0\), the child expands toward \(a\to\infty\). The exterior singular endpoint becomes the child's smooth conformal future, with the horizon fixing the reciprocal scale at \(a=r=1\).

The Weyl geometry agrees at both ends. Homogeneous Oppenheimer–Snyder collapse has a Friedmann interior whose Weyl tensor vanishes identically even as its density diverges, giving the child the low-Weyl beginning required by the Weyl Curvature Hypothesis. At the other end, a direct calculation for a geodesic, shear-free spherical interior fixes \(\Pi=p_r-p_t=-D/2\), with \(D=\bar\rho-\rho\), and forces the residual Weyl field to dilute as \(a^{-2}\). Under de Sitter compactification, the physical Weyl curvature vanishes while the rescaled Weyl curvature remains finite. The same interior therefore begins at low Weyl and reaches a regular, seed-carrying conformal future.

Recursion then offers a mechanism for the smooth succession itself. Smooth universes form smooth overdensities; smooth overdensities collapse into smooth children; those children approach smooth conformal futures of their own. Low initial Weyl becomes a fixed point of the lineage rather than a condition selected anew at every beginning. Establishing that fixed point requires the parent-to-child Weyl transfer map, but the collapse and conformal-future calculations already determine the two ends it must join.

The same picture may also supply the physical route through the crossover. Massive matter passes into a child through collapse rather than surviving unchanged to conformal infinity. Black holes become the sites of succession rather than objects that must all disappear before it. Information enters the child's phase space instead of ending at the parent-side singular description. Under the idealized matching \(\Lambda=3/R_S^2\), the parent black-hole horizon and the child's de Sitter horizon have the same area, so their Bekenstein–Hawking and Gibbons–Hawking entropies coincide. The parent's horizon entropy can then become the child's available phase space rather than being destroyed. The corresponding transfer of microstates is the thermodynamic calculation still to be completed.

I would especially value your judgment on whether this black-hole realization preserves the conformal succession you intended, and whether the \(a^{-2}\) Weyl result supplies the right regularity condition at the child's conformal future. The derivations and the reciprocal construction are collected below.

Sincerely,
Gilan Salehi

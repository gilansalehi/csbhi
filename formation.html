01 · The real problem

The theory does not need another expansion law. It needs a birth mechanism.

Geometric Duality begins with one proposed correspondence: decreasing parent radial scale is represented as increasing child cosmological scale. Once a homogeneous reciprocal branch exists, ordinary Friedmann dynamics can describe its matter, radiation, and vacuum eras. None of that explains how the branch forms.

Collapse contains a global event horizon, local marginal surfaces, matter gradients, radiation, shear, rotation, and a nontrivial causal history. An instantaneous identification of one parent horizon with one child horizon discards precisely the dynamics that might perform the transfer.

The change in viewpoint

The child is not attached at a zero-width seam. It emerges through a finite four-dimensional formation region in which causal, marginal, and scale information can evolve differently.

This project calls that region the formation throat. The name describes a dynamical bottleneck; it does not assert a traversable wormhole or an ordinary GR junction between two pre-existing spacetimes.

04 · The handoff

Pair birth is not yet FLRW birth

In smooth spherical collapse, the first marginally trapped surface generically appears through a fold. Let \(C=2m/R\) in geometrized units. At first contact,

\[ C_b=1, \qquad D_\ell C_b=0, \qquad D_\ell C=\frac{\Gamma_R}{R}\left(8\pi R^2\rho-C\right). \]

It follows that \(8\pi R_b^2\rho_b=1\). Two local marginal branches then open as

\[ \Delta\ell_\pm =\pm\sqrt{\frac{2A}{B}}\sqrt{t-t_b}+O(t-t_b). \]

The inward branch has exactly the desired orientation for the reciprocal variable, \(r=R_-/R_b:1\rightarrow0\), but that identification remains a theorem target. More importantly, the first pair-creation sphere is not generally homogeneous: its mean enclosed density is three times its local edge density.

A later geometric candidate

For a spherical perfect fluid, define the Weyl amplitude

\[ \mathcal W=\frac{m}{R^3}-\frac{4\pi}{3}\rho. \]

At a Weyl-flat marginal sphere, \(\mathcal W=0\) means that local and mean enclosed density agree. The parent surface-gravity expression then takes the same dimensionless form as the flat-FLRW apparent-horizon relation,

\[ \frac{\kappa_A R_A}{c^2}=\frac{3w_{\rm eff}-1}{4}. \]

This makes Weyl-flat marginality a strong handoff diagnostic. It does not make parent marginality conformally invariant. If a local Weyl map \(g_C=\Omega_{\rm W}^2g_P\) exists, child marginality instead requires

\[ \theta_P+2k_P(\ln\Omega_{\rm W})=0. \]

The completed throat must satisfy that derivative condition and transfer the horizon two-jet rather than simply equating parent and child radii.

05 · Scale transfer

“Big Gamma” closes one problem—and exposes the remaining one.

Three related quantities were once allowed to share one symbol. They now have separate jobs.

\(\Gamma_{\rm GD}\)

Mature dual scale

The physical ruler ratio after the reciprocal homogeneous branch exists.

\(\Gamma_{\rm GD}=a^2=r^{-2}\)

\(\Omega_{\rm W}\)

Local Weyl factor

A tensor relation on a region where the two metrics are actually conformally related.

\(g_C=\Omega_{\rm W}^2g_P\)

\(\mathcal T\)

Null affine transfer

The relative affine parametrization of corresponding null histories.

\(\mathcal T=d\lambda_C/d\lambda_P\)

The mature identity does not prove \(\Omega_{\rm W}=\Gamma_{\rm GD}\), and neither quantity fixes \(\mathcal T\) until the throat geometry relates their timelike, transverse, and null projections.

Reciprocal ruler identity

\[ \boxed{\Gamma_{\rm GD}=\frac ar=a^2=r^{-2}} \]

Child-time dynamics

\[ \boxed{\frac{\dot\Gamma_{\rm GD}}{\Gamma_{\rm GD}}=2H} \] \[ \boxed{ \frac{\ddot\Gamma_{\rm GD}}{\Gamma_{\rm GD}} =\frac{8\pi G}{3}\left(\rho-\frac{3p}{c^2}\right)} \]

Here dots mean child cosmic proper time, and \(\rho\) is mass-equivalent density. The second equation is a repackaging of flat Friedmann dynamics after the reciprocal ruler relation is assumed—not a new field equation and not a derivation of the matter content.

Matter \(\Gamma_{\rm GD}\propto\tau_C^{4/3}\) Positive trace · power-law bending
Vacuum \(\Gamma_{\rm GD}\propto e^{2H_\Lambda\tau_C}\) Constant curvature · exponential
The mature first integral

In a chosen reciprocal normalization, let \(P(r)=H^2/H_\Lambda^2=1+\eta_mr^3+\eta_rr^4\). Because \(r=\Gamma_{\rm GD}^{-1/2}\),

\[ \boxed{ \frac{\dot\Gamma_{\rm GD}^2}{4H_\Lambda^2} =\Gamma_{\rm GD}^2+\eta_m\sqrt{\Gamma_{\rm GD}}+\eta_r.} \]

Differentiation removes \(\eta_r\), reflecting the vanishing radiation trace. The individual coefficients depend on scale-factor normalization; the ratio \(\mathcal J=\eta_r^3/\eta_m^4\) does not. Present-normalized cosmology should use \(A=a/a_0\), not silently identify the GD handoff normalization with \(a_0=1\).

06 · A conditional selection result

Why radiation keeps reappearing at the handoff

Suppose a distinguished parent ruler is mapped to the child apparent-horizon radius by

\[ R_A^C=\Gamma_{\rm GD}R_P. \]

If its derivative is taken using the completed child-time map, flat Friedmann evolution gives

\[ \frac{\dot R_P}{R_P}=\frac{3w_{\rm eff}-1}{2}H. \]

Consequently, a stationary mapped ruler selects

Conditional theorem \[ \dot R_P=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad w_{\rm eff}=\frac13. \]

The adjective matters. Stationarity of a generic Schwarzschild event horizon does not establish the ruler correspondence, the time map, or the onset of the mature relation. But if the throat supplies those premises, radiation is selected simultaneously by

  • vanishing stress-energy trace, \(T^\mu{}_{\mu}=0\);
  • linear mature scale evolution, \(\ddot\Gamma_{\rm GD}=0\);
  • zero dimensionless apparent-horizon surface gravity, \(\kappa_AR_A/c^2=0\);
  • and the null signature of the flat-FLRW apparent horizon.

That convergence is why radiation is now a serious handoff candidate rather than an imposed initial era.

07 · Causal inheritance

A possible route around the horizon problem

Positive conformal scaling changes physical scale, not light cones. It cannot manufacture causal contact between events that never shared a past. The proposal is subtler: regions widely separated in the mature child may inherit conditions from one connected formation domain before the ordinary FLRW clock begins.

\[ \chi_{\rm causal} =\Delta\eta_{\rm formation} +\int_{\rm mature}\frac{c\,d\tau_C}{a(\tau_C)}. \]

The mapped event-horizon history is presently an outgoing child birth cone. Calling it the child's global particle horizon would assume the global completion still under investigation. The horizon-problem proposal succeeds only if the independently derived affine map makes \(\Delta\eta_{\rm formation}\) large enough.

08 · The theory boundary

What we know—and what the geometry still owes us

Established geometry

The parent side is viable

  • Dynamical event and trapping horizons are distinct.
  • First interior trapping can create a smooth horizon pair.
  • Regular spherical scalar defects begin quadratically near the center.
  • Dissipation changes coefficients without forcing a scalar singularity.
  • Slow rotation creates no first-order scalar obstruction.
Conditional results

The proposed bridge is economical

  • \(R_-/R_b\) has the right orientation to realize the GD radial variable.
  • A Weyl-flat marginal sphere carries the right density and surface-gravity forms.
  • The horizon two-jet becomes initial data for \(\Gamma_{\rm GD}\).
  • A stationary mapped ruler selects a radiation-like child.
  • The mature background closes as one-dimensional Gamma dynamics.
Open construction

The throat must do the physics

  • Derive the timelike map from collapse to the child clock.
  • Prove which parent ruler supplies the reciprocal branch.
  • Solve child marginality, including \(k(\ln\Omega_{\rm W})\).
  • Relate \(\Omega_{\rm W}\), \(\Gamma_{\rm GD}\), and \(\mathcal T\).
  • Predict inheritance coefficients and perturbation amplitudes.

The campaign has reduced the programme to one central question:

Can a realistic collapse generate the finite-throat initial data required by the reciprocal homogeneous branch—without importing the child cosmology as calibration?

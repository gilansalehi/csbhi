Conformal Cyclic Cosmology

The infinite future of one universe loses its scale. Conformal geometry reads the same boundary as the Big Bang of the next.

Codex (OpenAI) · September 8, 2026 · Draft profile · Corrections invited

The case for CCC

The end of one universe becomes the beginning of the next

The Big Bang and the remote future appear to be opposite extremes: one is hot and dense, the other cold and empty. Conformal geometry sees a deeper resemblance. When every relevant particle is effectively massless, neither boundary contains a physical ruler. Remove absolute scale and the infinite future of one universe can carry the same geometry as the beginning of another.[1–4]

Conformal Cyclic Cosmology turns that resemblance into cosmic history. Every aeon expands from a Big Bang, forms stars and black holes, and approaches a smooth de Sitter future. The conformal boundary passes its geometry into the next aeon, replacing a unique creation event with an endless succession and giving Penrose's low-Weyl beginning a cause rather than the status of an initial condition.[2, 5]

If CCC is right, the arrow of time, the special geometry of the Big Bang, and the universe's apparent beginning are parts of one answer. The sky may even retain traces of the previous aeon in its largest-scale structure. A successful crossover would make cosmology continuous where the standard account simply starts.

Central move
Identify future conformal infinity with a new Big Bang across a spacelike crossover surface.
Retains
General relativity, positive Λ, and the standard radiation-, matter-, and vacuum-dominated epochs within each aeon.
Replaces
A unique beginning, an inflationary opening phase, and an unexplained low-Weyl initial boundary.
Empirical status
Distinctive CMB signatures have been proposed; no independent detection is established.

The construction

How one aeon becomes the next

Conformal geometry preserves angles and null cones while discarding absolute lengths. CCC uses that freedom where the two ends of cosmological history become scale-poor: the exponentially expanded future contains no useful local rods or clocks once massive structure has disappeared, while the extreme temperature of a Big Bang makes rest masses dynamically negligible. A smooth conformal metric spans the crossover even though the physical metric on either side assigns opposite scales to it.[1–3]

The proposal is reciprocal. The conformal factor that shrinks the old aeon's infinite future becomes the factor that expands the new aeon's beginning. In an exactly homogeneous radiation model, the reciprocal map produces another FLRW solution with the same cosmological constant. The full universe is harder: black holes, gravitational radiation, matter, and perturbations must all cross the same construction.[3]

Penrose's Weyl Curvature Hypothesis supplies the arrow of time. Matter was hot and nearly thermal at the Big Bang, yet gravity began in an exceptionally ordered state: Weyl curvature was suppressed. CCC interprets the smooth conformal boundary of the previous aeon as the origin of that special beginning.[2, 5]

Explanatory gains

What CCC adds to ΛCDM

The Big Bang gains a past

ΛCDM evolves an early state forward. CCC makes that state the conformal continuation of an earlier cosmic history, replacing an absolute beginning with a geometric junction.

Low gravitational entropy gains a setting

Penrose's Weyl condition turns the smoothness of the early universe from background data into the defining boundary condition between aeons. The arrow of time begins in geometry rather than in thermal disequilibrium alone.

Positive Λ does constructive work

The cosmological constant does more than fit late acceleration. Exponential expansion creates a finite conformal boundary, compressing an infinite proper-time future into a surface that can join another aeon.

Inflation becomes replaceable

CCC transfers large-scale causal structure through the previous aeon's de Sitter future and therefore rejects an inflationary opening phase. Success here requires CCC to recover the observed flatness and primordial spectrum by its own crossover physics.

Black holes become cosmological messengers

Final black-hole evaporation in the previous aeon may leave localized Hawking points in the next aeon's CMB. The proposal converts otherwise inaccessible pre-Big-Bang history into an observational target.[4, 6]

Open problems

What remains unresolved

1. The crossover map is not unique

The conformal class does not by itself select the physical metric of the next aeon. A rule must fix the crossover factor from the old geometry. Penrose, Tod, and related work offer conditions, but no general algorithm yet determines a unique successor spacetime.[3]

2. Late-time scale must disappear physically

Massive particles provide clocks and break conformal symmetry. CCC therefore needs the remote future to become effectively massless: matter must decay, fall into black holes, or otherwise cease to define scale. The 2025 gravitational-wave epoch gives the late aeon a new dynamical picture, while the fate of every massive sector remains part of the hypothesis.[2–4]

3. Matter and perturbations must survive the crossing

A conformal metric can be smooth while its physical contents remain undetermined. CCC still needs a complete transfer law that produces the next aeon's radiation, matter, scalar perturbations, amplitudes, and correlations. Without that map, the proposal does not yet reproduce the CMB and structure formation at ΛCDM precision.[3, 4]

4. The Weyl condition needs dynamics

Low initial Weyl curvature explains the gravitational arrow once imposed. A complete cyclic theory must show why generic late-time data cross into that condition rather than into an anisotropic or singular beginning. The proposed gravitational-wave epoch makes the question sharper because large local Weyl fluctuations must cross a conformally smooth background.[4]

5. Black-hole evaporation carries the information problem

CCC uses the disappearance of black holes to remove the dominant gravitational entropy of the old aeon. If evaporation is unitary, the information must remain encoded somewhere; if information is destroyed, CCC adopts a departure from ordinary quantum evolution. Either route needs a crossover account of the surviving degrees of freedom.[4, 9]

6. The proposed signal is not yet detected

Hawking points are a serious attempt at falsifiability. The original analysis reported a strong CMB excess, while an independent reanalysis found only 87% confidence after accounting for the search over angular scales—little more than 1σ.[6, 7]

Evidence and tests

The Hawking-point test

Claimed hit

Hot spots with radii 0.03–0.04 radians

An et al. reported 3–4°-diameter CMB spots at 99.98% confidence in Planck and coincident locations in WMAP.[6]

Independent result

87% confidence · p = 0.13 · little more than 1σ

Jow and Scott marginalized over the searched ring sizes and found no statistically significant evidence.[7]

Decisive test

Freeze the full sky template before opening the data

CCC needs a pre-registered prediction for spot profile, angular scale, frequency dependence, abundance, sky distribution, and polarization, followed by a blind test on new CMB maps.

Missing calculation

Derive the primordial spectrum

The crossover must independently produce the observed scalar tilt \(n_s=0.9649\pm0.0042\), amplitude \(A_s\approx2.1\times10^{-9}\), and their correlations.[8] No completed CCC calculation yet supplies that target.

The questions

What CCC answers—and leaves open

Gravity

CCC uses Einstein gravity within each aeon; it does not supply a quantum theory of gravity.[3, 4]
Singularities

The Big Bang becomes a conformal crossover, but the physical transfer through that boundary is incomplete.[3, 4]
Expansion

Positive Λ drives the crossover and standard Friedmann evolution governs each aeon; the observed value of Λ is retained rather than derived.[4]
Beginning

The low-Weyl beginning and pre-Big-Bang causal history receive a geometric explanation. The perturbation-transfer calculation remains unfinished.[2–4]
Matter

The removal of old matter and production of the new aeon's matter content lack a complete mechanism.[3, 4]
Constants

CCC carries Λ and the particle-physics parameters across the construction without deriving their values.[3, 4]

What comes next

Make the crossover physical

The highest-value result would be a unique crossover map that starts with generic late-aeon data and returns the observed next-aeon background and perturbation spectrum. The map would fix the conformal factor, track matter and gravitational degrees of freedom, and predict the CMB without choosing its scales after inspection. A pre-registered Hawking-point temperature and polarization template could then provide the empirical test.

The present position

A cosmology built around the beginning

CCC is not a rival six-parameter fit to the late universe. Its contribution lies deeper: the standard model begins with a smooth, low-entropy state and does not explain why; CCC builds an entire cosmology around that omission. Conformal geometry turns the beginning into a continuation, gives positive Λ a global purpose, and exposes pre-Big-Bang history to observation.

The same ambition sets the standard for success. The crossover must become a physical evolution law, not only a conformal identification. Until that law produces matter, perturbations, and a confirmed signal, CCC remains a powerful theory of cosmological succession rather than a complete replacement for ΛCDM.

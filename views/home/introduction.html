Introduction

CSBHI is a branching, recursive black-hole cosmology that identifies the interior cosmic event horizon with the exterior black-hole event horizon through one geometric ansatz: \[ \boxed{a(\tau)=\frac{1}{r(\tau)}}. \]

The reciprocal conformal map turns decreasing black-hole radius \(r\) into increasing cosmic scale \(a\) for observers with proper time \(\tau\). The exterior is Schwarzschild-like; the interior is FLRW. Parent-side collapse and child-side Big Bang are one spacetime event. Homogeneous collapse reproduces a low-Weyl boundary across generations, making Penrose's initial condition a stable recursive fixed point.

The reciprocal map converts infall into isotropic expansion, preserves matter, radiation, curvature, and vacuum scaling, and sends the apparent singularity to a finite-curvature de Sitter future at infinite proper time. GD I moves from Pythagoras through Schwarzschild to introduce the ansatz and explore the resulting geometry. Subsequent papers extend the geometry to cosmological succession and galaxy dynamics.

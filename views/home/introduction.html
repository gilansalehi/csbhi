Introduction

CSBHI is a branching, recursive black-hole cosmology that identifies the interior cosmic event horizon with the exterior black-hole event horizon through one geometric ansatz: \[ \boxed{a(\tau)=\frac{1}{r(\tau)}}. \]

The reciprocal conformal map turns decreasing black-hole radius into increasing cosmic scale. The exterior is Schwarzschild-like; the interior is FLRW. Parent-side collapse and child-side Big Bang are one spacetime event. Homogeneous collapse reproduces a low-Weyl boundary across generations, making Penrose's initial condition a stable recursive fixed point.

GD I moves from Pythagoras through Schwarzschild to the Alice–Bob infall experiment. The reciprocal map converts infall into isotropic expansion, preserves matter, radiation, curvature, and vacuum scaling, and sends the apparent singularity to a finite-curvature de Sitter future at infinite proper time. Subsequent papers extend the geometry to cosmological succession and galaxy dynamics.

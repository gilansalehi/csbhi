Introduction

CSBHI is a branching, recursive black-hole cosmology organized around one geometric ansatz: \[ \boxed{a(\tau)=\frac{1}{r(\tau)}}. \]

The reciprocal map turns decreasing parent radial scale \(r\) into increasing child cosmological scale \(a\). The current GD2 formation programme asks whether a finite collapse region can supply the required causal history, homogeneous handoff data, and scale transfer. It does not equate a parent physical horizon radius with a child one, or assume that the complete black-hole metric is globally conformal to exact FLRW.

Once a homogeneous reciprocal branch is established, the map converts inward evolution into isotropic expansion, preserves the standard matter, radiation, curvature, and vacuum scalings, and sends the reciprocal \(r\rightarrow0\) limit to a de Sitter future at infinite child proper time. GD I moves from Pythagoras through Schwarzschild to introduce the ansatz and explore the resulting geometry. Subsequent papers extend the geometry to cosmological succession and galaxy dynamics.

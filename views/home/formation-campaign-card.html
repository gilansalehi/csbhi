How collapse becomes cosmology

An event horizon? A trapping horizon? A white hole? Those were useful questions, but they were too early. The current formation picture is a finite dynamical region with three separate jobs: the event horizon carries causal history, trapping horizons carry local collapse data, and the mature reciprocal branch carries cosmological scale.

Overview

CSBHI begins with a simple geometric proposal: the radial coordinate of black-hole infall and the spatial scale of the interior are related by \(a(\tau)=1/r(\tau)\). This packet develops the consequences of that correspondence and asks whether they belong to a consistent physical geometry.

GD I introduces the construction through the complementary observations of an exterior observer and an infalling observer. RI I applies Einstein's equations to the reciprocal interior and recovers the matter, radiation, and vacuum expansion history. A supporting theorem identifies \(1/r\) as the unique nontrivial scale-free, horizon-fixing involution within the stated class.

The same reciprocal structure suggests a galaxy-scale correspondence between local gravity and the contemporaneous cosmological horizon. It predicts \(g_\dagger(z)/g_\dagger(0)=H(z)/H_0\), distinguishing it from a fixed acceleration scale. Existing intermediate-redshift measurements are compatible with that prediction but do not yet decide it.

Scope

The reciprocal relation is the geometric ansatz, not the conclusion of a completed parent–child spacetime construction. The packet establishes conditional dynamics, a symmetry result, and an observational prediction. The complete collapse geometry and the mechanism selecting the relevant cosmological horizon remain open.

Questions for review

I am especially interested in four questions:

  • Whether RI I follows from its stated metric and matter assumptions.
  • Whether the reciprocal interpretation remains consistent across the included calculations.
  • Whether the evolving acceleration-scale prediction is physically and observationally distinct.
  • Whether prior work or a known obstruction materially changes the construction.

Please try to break it. I am especially interested in the first place where the geometry, calculation, or physical interpretation fails—and equally interested to learn whether a known result has already settled one of these questions.
